---
name: Shop campaign boundaries
description: Owner approval, dedicated paid campaign products and guest purchase limits.
---

Paid campaigns require owner approval of actual provider prices, exact rewards
and UTC dates; an example such as “10,000 gems for $5” is not an economy or pricing
decision. Preserve coins and propose existing cosmetic rewards unless the owner
explicitly chooses otherwise.

**Why:** The owner requested truthful timed offers, not invented prices, currencies
or scarcity. A seed price or connected provider is not evidence of a purchasable
store-approved product.

**How to apply:** Keep campaigns inactive when provider prices cannot be verified.
Use dedicated campaign SKUs so the standard catalog cannot bypass timing/limits;
native promotional cosmetics must be non-consumable and agree with the campaign
dates. Obtain separate approval before activating, charging or publishing.

Purchase limits are per server-issued guest credential, not per human or display
name. Device-local rewards remain separate from the verified online wallet.

**Why:** The product has local guest profiles, not verified cloud account linking.

**How to apply:** State this boundary to buyers. Never promise cross-device restore
or treat a matching display name as ownership proof.
