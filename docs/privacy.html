Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how GoldRush Arena handles information when you use the app.

Information handled by the app

Most game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. GoldRush Arena does not ask for your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Online features may send a randomly generated player identifier, display name, scores, event progress, challenge contributions, or gameplay clips to our servers. These features include leaderboards, live events, community challenges, and shared clips.

When you make or restore an in-app purchase, Apple and our purchase service provider, RevenueCat, process transaction details such as the product purchased, transaction status, storefront, and an anonymous app-user identifier. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

How information is used

  • to provide game progress, leaderboards, events, clips, and purchases;
  • to prevent abuse and troubleshoot problems;
  • to understand whether game features work as intended; and
  • to comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers needed to run the app, including Apple for App Store purchases, RevenueCat for purchase verification and entitlement management, and hosting providers used for online game features. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Retention and choices

Local information remains until you reset it, remove the app, or clear app data. Server records are kept only as long as reasonably needed to provide and protect the service. You can avoid online sharing by not using online features. To ask about or request deletion of server-side information associated with your player identifier, contact us.

Children

GoldRush Arena is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to goldrush.arena@gmail.com.
