Privacy disclosure

Pre-release draft. The publisher must review its legal identity, retention practices, service configuration and public policy address before release. This is not a claim of legal compliance.

Local player data

Player names, selected avatars, progress, inventory, settings, purchase-fulfillment records and saved clips are stored on the device. Device profiles are not authenticated cloud accounts. Signing out does not delete a profile, and deleting app data can erase progress. General cloud recovery is not promised.

Online features

Supported online matches use server-issued guest identifiers and send display names, appearances, party membership, latency and paddle inputs to the game server. The server stores credential hashes, skill values, earned online totals and completed match receipts. Native guest credentials use secure device storage; browser credentials use local origin storage. These are not verified email accounts or general cloud saves. Other online features can send challenge scores, referral information and intentionally shared clips. Hosting providers also receive network information needed to process requests. Active rooms and parties may be lost on restart; completed online receipts persist.

Optional device access

Avatar features may request camera or photo-library access when you choose to use them. You can decline access. Playback does not require microphone permission. Review any selected photo or clip before sharing it.

Optional event notifications

The installed iPhone/Android app can schedule local notifications for selected open events after you explicitly allow notification permission. Event choices, lead time and a random per-profile reminder identifier are stored on the device; no phone number or remote push token is collected for this feature. The operating system stores upcoming notification times and can display event text on your lock screen. Turn reminders off or clear them in Events; signing out cancels pending alerts, and deleting a profile removes its saved reminder settings. Device settings, Focus and battery restrictions may delay or hide alerts. Reopen Events for new windows or timezone changes. Event changes cannot revoke an alert while the app remains closed. The browser has in-app reminders only; SMS and background browser push are not enabled.

Optional AI design help

Paddle Studio sends your prompt and current design parameters to OpenAI through Replit AI Integrations. Avoid personal information in prompts. Saved custom designs belong to your guest identity and are not published to a marketplace. Provider processing policies apply.

Photo avatars

Pressing Save photo uploads through a size-limited authenticated server endpoint to cloud storage under your guest identity. Photos are private by default. Explicit publication makes a sanitized photo available to opponents. Revocation cannot erase copies already downloaded. Images are re-encoded without camera/location metadata. Use Remove saved photo in Profile to revoke access and request file deletion. Deleting a player also revokes its photo and guest credential before clearing local access; online profiles need a connection to complete deletion. Uninstalling alone does not delete server photos. A scheduled process removes expired staging uploads independently of guest activity; unsuccessful file cleanup may require maintenance.

Purchases

Web checkout uses Stripe; native digital purchases use the app store and RevenueCat when configured. Providers process product identifiers, transaction and entitlement details, and app-user identifiers. The app does not receive full payment-card details. Opening checkout or cancelling it is not a completed purchase.

Sharing and service providers

Sharing is optional. A share sheet is not proof of publication. Information may be processed by the hosting, store, payment and sharing providers needed for the features you choose. Their own policies apply. No advertising or sale of player information is intended in this pre-release build.

Choices and retention

Settings provides device-profile management and support. Device deletion does not prove server-side deletion. Contact the publisher about server data, retention and deletion requests. Retention periods and age-related obligations must be confirmed before public release.
