## Description

FOUR PADDLES ENTER. ONE SURVIVES.

GoldRush Arena turns air hockey into a four-way elimination battle. Guard your side of the arena, read every rebound, and keep the ball from getting past you. Lose your lives and you're out. Outlast the field to take the win.

Matches are quick, controls are simple, and the pace keeps climbing.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Jump into fast matches against computer-controlled rivals
• Try Classic, Blitz, Chaos, Sudden Death, Survival, Pinball, and more
• Use one-finger paddle controls designed for portrait play
• Keep playing when you're offline; online features appear when a connection is available

BUILD YOUR LEGACY
• Rise through 23 competitive ranks
• Follow a 25-stop Trophy Road full of useful rewards
• Upgrade relics and charge powerful abilities during a match
• Complete daily challenges, collect achievements, and chase win streaks

MAKE THE ARENA YOURS
• Unlock paddle skins, trails, themes, and other cosmetics
• Save and share highlight clips from your best moments
• Join a party with friends using a short party code
• Restore eligible App Store purchases at any time from the Shop

No forced account creation. No advertising network. Just a sharp, neon arcade game that is easy to start and difficult to put down.

Enter the arena and see how long you can hold your wall.
