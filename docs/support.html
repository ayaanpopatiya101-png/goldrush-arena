When reporting a problem

Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, the screen where it happened and steps to reproduce it. Share a screenshot if useful. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other credentials.

Purchases and progress

For purchase issues, provide the provider's transaction reference, not financial credentials. Do not clear app data while troubleshooting: player saves and consumables can be device-local. A local save is not a cloud backup.
