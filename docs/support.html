Support

Need help with a purchase, lost progress, or something that does not look right? We are happy to help.

Email support

goldrush.arena@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases in the Shop to restore eligible non-consumable items. Consumable coin packs cannot be restored after they have been delivered. Apple handles billing and refund requests through the App Store.

Progress

Most progress is stored on the device. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove local progress, so please contact us before reinstalling when troubleshooting.
